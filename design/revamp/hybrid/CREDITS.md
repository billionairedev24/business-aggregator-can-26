# Photo credits

The photos in `img/` used by the “Proposed” mockups are copied unchanged from `design/revamp/img/` (the S-166 direction set). All come from the Open Images Dataset V7 (Google), which collects Flickr photos published under Creative Commons Attribution 2.0. They were resized to at most 800 px wide and saved as JPEG at quality 75; the mockups crop them with CSS `object-fit`. People photos come without model releases: they are for this design review only and need replacing with released stock or merchant photos before any public use.
