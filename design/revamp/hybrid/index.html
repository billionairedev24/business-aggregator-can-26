+What changes

  • Consumer header, department strip and footer go deep violet #1D1442 / #2A1E5C
  • Home opens with Chinook’s hero band: “Everything Kensington needs, tonight.”, a trust line, and three photo tiles (Book a pro · Shop local · Order in) that straddle the violet edge
  • The cut-off strip becomes a run card in the hero, with a yellow countdown sticker
  • Main Street’s maker of the week follows, then Plainsight’s tiles and rails
  • Violet #5B34F0 is the action colour everywhere: buttons, links, focus, in all three apps
  • Deals turn yellow #FFD23F with ink text, set as slightly tilted stickers with a hard ink shadow
  • Archivo expanded 800 for hero and marketing headlines; IBM Plex Sans stays for every UI string
  • Studio and console get a violet current-page marker and a violet focus ring

=What stays (Plainsight)

  • Selected states in solid ink #121418: segments, chips, checkboxes, tabs, pager
  • Shapes: 3 px controls, 4 px panels, 2 px status tags; buttons, underline tabs, joined segments and square chips never look alike
  • Density: 5 results per row, 7 per rail, left filter rail with counts
  • Product on white: every photo in a 1:1 grey well with a 12 px inset
  • Status colours: success #137A3A, warning #8F5600, danger #B42318, info #1F5FA8
  • Red for Suspend and every destructive action
  • Studio and console chrome stays white; line #C8CDD5, well #F3F4F6

Contrast · WCAG 2.2 AA

Every text and background pair the proposal introduces, worked out with the WCAG relative-luminance formula. Text needs 4.5:1, large text and UI edges 3:1.

  • Yellow is never text on white. #FFD23F on white is 1.44:1, so the old commerce-text (#9A1270 text) cannot become yellow text. On white it becomes an ink-on-yellow pill (12.77:1: “Order within 2 h 48 min”, “Ends in 6 h 12 min”). On the violet strip, yellow text is fine (“Today’s deals”, 10.11:1).
  • The yellow sticker gets a 1.5 px hard ink shadow. The sticker’s text is what passes; the ink edge keeps the sticker readable on a white or pale photo well, where yellow against white is only 1.44:1.
  • No violet text on violet. #5B34F0 on #1D1442 is only 2.6:1, so links and outlines on the violet chrome use white or #B9A8FF (8.21:1). The violet search button is judged against the white field it is joined to (6.57:1).
  • Header meta lightened. Chinook’s #B7AEDB becomes #C3BBE6 (9.40:1) so 11.5 px “Deliver to” and “Hello, sign in” read comfortably. #5B34F0 itself passes unchanged: white on it is 6.57:1, ink on #FFD23F is 12.77:1.
