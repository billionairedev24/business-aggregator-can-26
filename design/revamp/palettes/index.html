Bow River

Glacier-fed petrol chrome, a clear river-blue action and coral stickers. Fresh, quick, modern.

  • #0B2E38
  • #12414D
  • #006F8E
  • #FF7E61
  • #FF9A80

Contrast decisions

  • Coral #FF7E61 carries ink text (7.38:1). It is 2.50:1 on white, so it never becomes text on white; on cards it is always the tilted sticker with the 1.5 px ink edge.
  • The hero accent and the strip’s deal label use a lighter coral #FF9A80, because #FF7E61 on the strip would be 4.45:1, just under 4.5. On the strip it now measures 5.39:1.
  • The river-blue action #006F8E sits at hue 193°, between the petrol chrome and info blue #1F5FA8 (hue 212°). Info stays a tinted tag, never a button.

Maple Ink

Charcoal chrome, a confident cobalt action and crimson maple stickers. Sharp, editorial, unmistakably local.

  • #17191D
  • #25282E
  • #1F5AD6
  • #D81E3C
  • #FF7A6B

Contrast decisions

  • Deals are crimson #D81E3C (hue 350°) with white text, 5.04:1. Danger stays brick #B42318 (hue 4°). Danger is a flat button or a tinted tag; a deal is always a tilted sticker with the ink edge, so the two don’t share a shape.
  • Maple red is never text on the charcoal: #D81E3C is only 3.50:1 there, so the hero accent and the strip’s deal label use the lighter #FF7A6B (6.91:1 and 5.80:1).
  • The deal tint becomes #FBE7EA, a cooler pink than danger’s tint #FCE8E6, and it never sits beside a danger message. The mark turns red with a white highlight dot.

Prairie Night

Midnight-blue chrome, an ultramarine action and sun-yellow stickers. Big sky after dark, rich and calm.

  • #0D1B4B
  • #172A68
  • #3346D3
  • #FFCE3A
  • #FFD13F

Contrast decisions

  • Not Amazon: the chrome is a saturated midnight #0D1B4B (blue channel 75, against #131921’s near-grey 33) and the strip is #172A68, not #232F3E. The yellow #FFCE3A sits at hue 45°, far from orange #FF9900 (hue 36°). Buttons are ultramarine, never yellow or orange.
  • Sun yellow works both as a sticker (ink 12.42:1) and as text on the chrome (11.32:1), so the hero accent and the deal label share one family.
  • The ultramarine #3346D3 is the strongest action of the four: 7.10:1 with white and 6.21:1 on its nav tint.

Recommendation

Pick Northern Pine. Bow River is the runner-up.

Pine changes the most about how Northline feels while changing the least about how it works. Forest green and harvest gold read as Calgary and the foothills rather than as a tech brand, and the pair sits well away from Amazon’s navy and orange. The gold stickers keep the deal mechanic the owner already approved: same tilt, same ink edge, ink text at 10.34:1. Every pair passes AA with room to spare.

One thing to watch: the spruce action (#0E6B58) and success green (#137A3A) are both greens. They sit 25° of hue apart, and success only ever appears as a tinted tag with an icon, never as a solid button. Check that in the Studio component gallery before you lock it in.

  1. Northern Pine: warmest and most local, and the most distinct from Amazon and other marketplaces. The gold reads as “deal” at a glance.
  2. Bow River: the cleanest system. The action, the deal and every status colour each get their own hue, so nothing can be mistaken for anything else. It is cooler and less warm than Pine.
  3. Prairie Night: the smallest step from today, since it keeps the yellow stickers and the highest contrast (7.10:1 on buttons). But midnight blue with yellow tags is the Best Buy and Flipkart formula, so it has the weakest identity of the four.
  4. Maple Ink: sharp and editorial, but crimson deals keep a red next to danger red. The sticker shape keeps them apart, but every new screen will have to keep checking that.
