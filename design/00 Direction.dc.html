Spruce & Honey

Local, fast, and warm to the touch.

A clean, dense utility interface — the speed of a modern delivery app — set on a warm off-white ground with a deep spruce brand and honey for the single action that matters. Serif headings give it a local, trusted voice; a sans body keeps it quick to scan.

Colour — five base tokens, everything derived

Edit the five values at the top of theme/northline.css (in code: packages/tokens/tokens.json). Ramps, borders, shadows, hover and pressed states are computed from them with color-mix(in oklch), so one change re-themes every screen.
