Northline brings the shops and service providers near you into one bilingual app.

SHOP LOCAL
• Browse local stores by department and fill one cart from several shops.
• Get it delivered on the evening’s pooled run.
• Follow your order from packed to delivered, and confirm when it arrives.

BOOK TRUSTED SERVICES
• Find cleaners, plumbers, mechanics and more, with their reviews, prices and next available times.
• Book a fixed-price visit in a few taps, or ask for a quote. Every quote is itemized, with the scope, exclusions, warranty and deposit shown up front.
• See when your provider is on the way, then sign off when the job is done.

PAY WITH CONFIDENCE
• Your payment is held and released to the business only after the job is complete or your order is delivered.
• Cards are entered in Stripe’s secure payment sheet. Northline never sees or stores your card number.
• Something wrong? Report a problem from the order and follow your refund case.

MADE FOR YOU
• English and French throughout.
• Browse as a guest; sign in to book and pay.
• Your location is used only while the app is open, to show what serves your address, and is never shared with businesses.
• Notifications for your orders, bookings and quotes, with quiet hours you choose.
• No ads and no tracking.

Northline is opening province by province. If we don’t serve your area yet, join the waitlist in the app.
