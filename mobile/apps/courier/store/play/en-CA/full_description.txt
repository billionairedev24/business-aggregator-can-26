Northline Courier is the app for couriers who deliver with Northline. You need a Northline courier account to sign in.

ON A SHIFT
• Start and end your shift.
• See your run’s stops in order, with the address, what to pick up and any delivery instructions.
• Pick up at each shop and mark what you collected.
• Drop off with proof of delivery: a photo at the door, the customer’s signature or their delivery PIN.
• Get a notification when you are given a new run or your run changes.

BUILT FOR THE ROAD
• Works on weak networks: your steps are saved on the phone and sent as soon as you are back online.
• English and French.

YOUR PRIVACY
• While you are on a run, the app shares your location so customers can follow their order on its way, also when the app is in the background. Only your latest position is kept, never a history, and sharing stops when your run ends.
• The camera is used only to photograph a delivery as proof.
• No ads and no tracking.
