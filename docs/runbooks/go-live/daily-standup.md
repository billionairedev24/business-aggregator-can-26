# Hypercare daily stand-up (template, en / fr)

15 minutes every morning of the 14 hypercare days ([../go-live.md § Hypercare](../go-live.md#hypercare)). Copy the
block into the launch channel or the day's notes; the numbers come from Grafana `northline-hypercare` (the market
selected) and Console › Go-live / UAT / Support.

## English

```text
Hypercare day [n] of 14 — [market] — [date]
On call today: primary [name] · secondary [name] · business contact [name]

Last 24 h (northline-hypercare)
- Orders: [n] · bookings: [n] · payouts sent / failed: [n] / [n]
- Checkout failed share: [x] % · sign-in failures: [n] · paging alerts: [n]
- SLO budget left (30 d): checkout [x] % · sign-in [x] % · payouts [x] %
- Support tickets from the market: [n] · UAT feedback: [n] (blocking open: [n])

Incidents and rollback criteria: [none / what happened, status]
Top issues (owner, due): 1. … 2. … 3. …
Businesses: [who needs a call today]
Exit criteria: [on track / which one is not, why]
Decisions / asks: …
```
